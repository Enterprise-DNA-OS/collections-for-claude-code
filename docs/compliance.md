# Evidence checks and their limits

Sources opened 6 October 2026. This command assists a trained registrar. It does not certify legal compliance, accreditation, safe treatment or permission to export.

| Rule | Evidence checked | Source and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| SPECTRUM-LOCATION | Current and normal locations, append-only movement records with mover and authoriser; insurance dates on loans | [Collections Trust: location and movement control](https://collectionstrust.org.uk/resource/location-and-movement-control-the-spectrum-standard/). The standard covers dated movements, accountability and insurance. Our checks do not assess environmental monitoring, packing, transport risk, suitability or the museum's full policies. |
| NZ-PROTECTED-OBJECTS | For overseas loans leaving NZ, each object needs a recorded assessment and reviewer; an object marked as requiring permission also needs a permission reference | [Manatū Taonga: apply for export permission](https://www.mch.govt.nz/our-work/protected-objects/exporting-protected-objects/apply-permission-export-protected-object-new). Consult the Ministry before applying. The registrar decides whether the Act applies. No automatic age classification, validity check or permit application occurs. Other restrictions, including CITES, remain separate. |
| LOCAL-EXPORT | Other international loans need the same evidence fields | Local workflow only. Not an implementation of Australian or other national export law. Consult the relevant authority. |
| LOCAL-TITLE | Object has an accepted accession linked to dated title evidence | Museum policy in this demo, not a legal determination of ownership. |
| LOCAL-LOAN | Signed agreement reference, nonempty object list, return dates | Museum policy. Loan checkout also requires an accepted acquisition and a condition review covering the due date. |
| LOCAL-POLICY | Inventory observation within 365 days; poor, urgent, absent or due condition reports; overdue loan | Demo policy intervals, not statutory periods. Change with /customise to the museum's policy. |

All objects are restricted by default. A rights note is an observation, not publication consent. The software never publishes images, taonga information or exact storage locations. It does not implement per-user cultural permissions. Configure access and consult the relevant communities before loading restricted material into any shared or model-connected environment.

The museum must verify the original loan agreement, insurance schedule and permission documents. A text reference can be wrong or out of date. The command cannot authenticate it. Any actual dispatch decision remains with the registrar.
